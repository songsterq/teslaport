What it is

A private door between two screens.

01

Encrypted end to end

Your phone encrypts the link and only the car can open it. The relay moves opaque bytes it has no key for.

02

No app, no account

Two web pages and a QR code. Nothing to install on the phone, nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into.

03

Nothing is stored

The server keeps no database. It fans each link straight through to the car and then forgets it completely.

How it works

Pair once, then send all you like.

Step 01

Open it on the car

The car screen shows a QR code and a short pairing code. Bookmark the page while you're there.

Step 02

Scan with your phone

Scanning pairs the two devices. The key rides in the link's fragment, which never reaches the server.

Step 03

Send a link

Paste, tap send, and watch it appear on the car. Tap it there to open it in the car's browser.
