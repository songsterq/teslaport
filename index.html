How it works

Pair once, then share links with your Tesla all you like.

Step 01

Open it on the car

The car screen shows a QR code and a short pairing code. Bookmark the page while you're there.

Step 02

Scan with your phone

Scanning pairs the two devices. The key rides in the link's fragment, which never reaches the server.

Step 03

Send a link

Paste, tap send, and watch it appear on the car. Tap it there to open it in the car's browser.

What it is

A private door between your phone and your Tesla.

01

Encrypted end to end

Your phone encrypts the link and only the car can open it. The relay moves opaque bytes it has no key for.

02

No app, no account

Two web pages and a QR code. Nothing to install on the phone, nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into.

03

Nothing is stored

The server keeps no database. It fans each link straight through to the car and then forgets it completely.

FAQ

Questions about Tesla link sharing.

What is Tesla link sharing?

Tesla link sharing means opening a page you found on your phone on the car's screen instead of retyping the address into the car's browser. TeslaPort does it over an encrypted relay.

How do I send a link from my phone to my Tesla?

Open TeslaPort on the car's browser, scan the QR code with your phone, then paste any URL and tap send. It appears on the car screen a second later.

Do I need an app or a Tesla account?

No. TeslaPort is two web pages and a QR code — nothing to install on the phone, nothing to sign up for, and it never touches your Tesla account.

Does this work with Tesla's built-in browser?

Yes. It is built for that browser: the car page is plain HTML and JavaScript, and a link you tap opens in the same tab the browser is already showing.

Is sharing a link to my Tesla private?

Your phone encrypts each link and only the paired car can decrypt it. The relay moves opaque bytes it holds no key for, and stores nothing.

Which Tesla models does this work with?

Any Tesla whose touchscreen has the web browser. Nothing here is model-specific — if the browser loads the page and the QR code renders, pairing works.
